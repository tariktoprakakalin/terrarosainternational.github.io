Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2025

1. Who we are

TerraRosa International operates as an independent commodity sourcing and trade network, focused on gold, silver and petrochemicals. This policy describes how we handle information submitted through this website (terrarosainternational.com).

2. What we collect and why

We only collect information that is necessary to evaluate and respond to trade inquiries:

  • Company name and country — to understand the counterparty profile.
  • Corporate e-mail address — to respond to your inquiry.
  • Product, quantity, port and bank profile details — to assess whether a match is feasible within our network.
  • Additional notes — any context you choose to provide.

We do not collect personal consumer data. All interactions on this site are business-to-business (B2B) in nature.

3. How we use your information

Information submitted through inquiry forms is used solely to:

  • Evaluate the technical and operational feasibility of the requested commodity transaction.
  • Contact you with a relevant response or request for clarification.
  • Maintain a record of inquiry correspondence for operational purposes.

We do not sell, rent or trade your information to third parties. We do not use submitted data for advertising or unsolicited marketing.

4. AI chat assistant

This site provides an optional AI assistant to help you pre-fill the inquiry form. Messages submitted through the chat are sent to our backend API for processing. These messages are handled with the same confidentiality as form submissions and are not retained beyond operational necessity.

5. Analytics and third-party services

We use the following third-party services for site operation and analytics:

  • Google Analytics (GA4) — collects anonymised usage data (page views, session counts) to help us understand how the site is used. No personally identifiable information is shared with Google Analytics.
  • Google Tag Manager — used to manage analytics scripts. It does not independently collect data.
  • Usercentrics CMP — a consent management platform that allows you to control which tracking technologies are active on your browser.

You may refuse or withdraw consent for analytics via the Usercentrics consent banner at any time.

6. Cookies

This site uses a minimal set of cookies. Strictly necessary cookies (e.g., theme preference stored in localStorage) require no consent. Analytics cookies are only activated after your explicit consent via the Usercentrics consent interface.

7. Data retention

Inquiry data is retained only as long as necessary for the evaluation and potential execution of the requested transaction, or as required by applicable commercial law. Inquiries that do not proceed to an active transaction are deleted within a reasonable period.

8. Your rights

Under applicable data protection law (including GDPR where relevant), you have the right to:

  • Access the information we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of inaccurate data.
  • Withdraw consent for analytics at any time via the Usercentrics interface.

To exercise these rights, contact us at: satis@terrarosainternational.com

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect submitted information. Form data is transmitted over HTTPS. We do not store payment or banking credentials.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
