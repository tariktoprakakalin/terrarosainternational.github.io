Certifications & compliance

In agriculture and energy sourcing engagements, the following documentation types are verified per transaction:

  • Product specifications and quality certificates (e.g. SGS, Bureau Veritas)
  • Certificate of Origin and phytosanitary documentation
  • Energy sector compliance and regulatory permits
  • INCOTERMS-aligned delivery and insurance documentation
  • Counterparty due diligence records

Certificates and technical documentation are evaluated with the relevant parties according to the product, sector and transaction scope.
