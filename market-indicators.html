Data sources and methodology

The first release is designed to use publicly available series from the World Bank Pink Sheet, U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA), U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) and FAO.

Update frequency

Series will be updated according to their source publication schedule, primarily monthly.

Data coverage

Unit, currency, date and source information will be shown with every chart.
